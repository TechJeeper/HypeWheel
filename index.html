Quick Start Guide

Welcome to HypeWheel! A highly customizable, performant spinning wheel app for picking random names or options.

  • Add Options: Click on a wheel's text area to enter names, one per line.
  • Customize: Use the gear icon () on a wheel to change colors, add custom images, and set spin speed/duration.
  • Spin: Click the "SPIN" button or the wheel to pick a random option.
  • Multiple Wheels: Click "+ Add New Wheel" in the top header to run multiple wheels simultaneously.
  • Theater Mode: Click the maximize icon () to expand a wheel to full screen.
  • Winners: View past winners by clicking the trophy icon in the top header.
